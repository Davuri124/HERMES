# HERMES 🧠📖

## Human-Centered Emotional Reader-Observer Modeling System for Multilingual Literary Interpretation

**Built on the QuLit Framework · H1–H7 · GPU-Validated on T4**

> **Does a poem mean the same thing to everyone who reads it?**

HERMES is a research system designed to computationally model a fundamental property of literary interpretation:

**the meaning of a literary text can change depending on who is reading it.**

A verse about longing may evoke grief in one reader, romantic love in another, spiritual devotion in another, and philosophical reflection in someone else.

HERMES attempts to represent this phenomenon mathematically by modeling not only the literary text, but also the **observer** reading it.

The observer is represented through:

- cultural background,
- emotional context,
- and historical period.

The system then combines this observer representation with multilingual textual representations to produce **reader-conditioned meaning**.

HERMES is not designed to determine a single "correct" interpretation of a poem.

Instead, it asks a different question:
